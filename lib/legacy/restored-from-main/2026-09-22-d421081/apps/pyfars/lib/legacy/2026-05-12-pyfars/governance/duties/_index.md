Duty records encode constraints that attach to power exercises.
When an office exercises power X, duty Y fires — the required action must be completed or the exercise is a substrate violation.

Duties are Type N→N closure rules in PredWork's normative-operational layering: antecedent reads normative cells (the power is held), consequent writes normative cells (the duty-active cell is set), which then gates the operational event via N→O rules.

Current duties: - [`consult-predwork-before-encoding`](consult-predwork-before-encoding.md) — fires on all record creation/modification. - [`derive-from-theory-before-planning`](derive-from-theory-before-planning.md) — fires on milestone and goal creation; stronger than the above. - [`maintain-roadmap-enrichment-discipline`](maintain-roadmap-enrichment-discipline.md) — fires on any planning-record creation/modification AND on milestone advancement to `soon`/`doing`; ensures fidelity-level matches horizon AND roadmap reach grows in step with promotion per the [roadmap-enrichment](../../concepts/roadmap-enrichment.md) concept.
Governs integration-stage discipline only. - [`run-full-cycle-not-just-integration`](run-full-cycle-not-just-integration.md) — fires on any roadmap-enrichment-cycle invocation; requires all five stages to run in order with substrate witnesses.
