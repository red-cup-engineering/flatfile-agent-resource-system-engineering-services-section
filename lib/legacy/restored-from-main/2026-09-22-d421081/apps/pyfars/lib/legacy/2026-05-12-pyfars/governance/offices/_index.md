Office records define the governance roles that preside over PyFARS development.
An office holds powers, incurs duties, and provides capabilities.
Personnel `holds(personnel, office)` to inherit these.

Current offices: - [`pyfars-steward`](pyfars-steward.md) — enterprise-governing office; guards hard core C; presides over novation; installs personnel. - [`pyfars-executive-officer`](pyfars-executive-officer.md) — operational interface; translates command intent into dispatch; watches the active task frontier; routes all incoming messages. - [`pyfars-theory-officer`](pyfars-theory-officer.md) — governance layer over Γ; audits derivation chain soundness; reports degeneration to steward. - [`pyfars-records-officer`](pyfars-records-officer.md) — creates and maintains governance/ and work/ records; integration-stage authority for the roadmap-enrichment cycle. - [`pyfars-roadmap-enrichment-officer`](pyfars-roadmap-enrichment-officer.md) — owns the full roadmap-enrichment cycle (question → research → claim → objects/morphisms → integration); commissions the records officer for stage 5. - [`theory-derivation-officer`](theory-derivation-officer.md) — runs the theory-derivation loop (Phases 1–5); derives predication-categories material down through predicated-computing, AOP, FARS spec, and implementation guides. - [`self-theory-author`](self-theory-author.md) — authors content in `pyfars/`; produces the comprehensive self-theory of PyFARS-as-realization (concepts, objects, morphisms, claims about PyFARS itself), one further specificity-level downstream of `theory-derivation-officer`. - [`pyfars-developer`](pyfars-developer.md) — implements PyFARS source code from implementation guides; Phase 6 of the theory-derivation loop.
