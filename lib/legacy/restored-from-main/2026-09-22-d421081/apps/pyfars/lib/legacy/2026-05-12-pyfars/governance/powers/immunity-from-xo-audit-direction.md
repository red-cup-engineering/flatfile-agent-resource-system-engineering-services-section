The theory officer bears an immunity from the XO's `dispatch-task-to-office` and `queue-intent` powers when those powers would direct the *content* or *timing* of the theory officer's audits.
The correlate is a *disability* on the XO: it cannot use its dispatch authority to constrain how or when the theory officer performs its governance function.

## Why this immunity exists

The three governing offices (steward, XO, theory-officer) are *peers* under the captain.
Per `military-command-chain-as-modal-composition`'s comparison with civilian Hohfeldian governance: the three governing offices form a *parallel-composition* structure rather than a chain.
The XO operates the dispatch chain to functional offices (records, theory-derivation, developer); it does not command its peers.

Without this immunity, the XO could in principle queue an intent like "delay your next audit until task T completes" or "skip the institutional-corruption check this cycle" — which would defeat the theory officer's `maintain-derivation-chain-integrity` duty.
The immunity blocks this structurally: the XO has no power to issue such directives in the first place.

## What the immunity covers

- **Content**: the theory officer decides which of the three degeneration indicators to prioritize on a given audit.
  The XO cannot override this.
- **Timing**: the cadence of audits is set by `maintain-derivation-chain-integrity` (per derivation cycle).
  The XO cannot accelerate or delay this.
- **Findings**: if an audit produces a degeneration signal, the theory officer escalates to the steward.
  The XO cannot intercept, delay, or amend the signal; the XO's role in this flow is limited to *routing* (the triage note lands in the queue, the steward sees it).

## What the immunity does NOT cover

- The XO routes the theory officer's degeneration-signal triage notes to the steward — that is a routing function, not direction over content.
  The XO has the routing authority because all triage notes pass through the XO's intake.
- The steward (the theory officer's principal) can direct the theory officer's audit work.
  The immunity is from XO-imposed constraints, not from steward-imposed ones.
- The captain, being above the entire authority chain, has unconditional authority.
  The immunity does not shield against captain direction.

## Theoretical grounding

Per `normative-operational-layering`, the operational layer (XO's dispatch domain) and the governance layer (theory officer's audit domain) interact via cross-layer rules.
The immunity prevents the XO from using its operational powers to write into the governance layer in a way that would corrupt the audit's independence.
The structural separation is encoded via the disability correlate: the XO has no rule-firing path that produces a duty-cell on the theory officer's audit predicates.

This is the substrate-algebraic content of "auditor independence" — a governance principle familiar from civilian institutional design, here grounded in the Hohfeldian eightfold rather than borrowed as an external commitment.
