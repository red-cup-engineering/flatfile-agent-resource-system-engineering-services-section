## Reading the map

- **NORTH** is up the page.
  The mainland and the ferry terminal are off the south edge.
- **Mt Farstone** dominates the island's center.
  Streams from its flanks form **the Farr River** (south) and feed **The Marsh** (northeast).
- **Town of Farston** wraps the harbor on the south coast: **downtown** at the river mouth, **harbor district** at the ferry terminal, **the Ridge** climbing the mountain's southwest shoulder, **Marsh-End** stretching toward the salt marsh.
- **Farr Valley** is the agricultural strip in the river's mid-course.
- **North Shore** is the rural strip along the north coast.
- **South Point Light** marks the harbor approach.
