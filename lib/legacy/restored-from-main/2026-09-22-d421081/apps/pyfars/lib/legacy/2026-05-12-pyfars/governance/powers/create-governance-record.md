Admits the holder to create a new file under `governance/`.
Covers: decisions, conventions, charter amendments, office records, power records, duty records.

## Attached duties

- `consult-predwork-before-encoding` fires before any use of this power.

## Does not cover

- Creating files under `work/` (separate power: `create-work-record`).
- Modifying existing governance records (separate power: `modify-governance-record`).
