The agricultural river valley running from Mt Farstone's southern flanks down to the upper edge of town — ~140 ha of cleared farmland organized into ~22 farmsteads.
Hay, vegetable row crops, two small orchards, four operating dairies.
Population about 350.

Farr Valley sits outside the town's chartered municipal boundary but inside the polity's substrate scope; land-use authority is incorporated through county-style records (forthcoming).
Public Works's water-quality inspectors sample three Farr Valley well sites quarterly per Charter Article III §4.

The Farr River traverses the valley; the riparian buffer ordinance is observed by all but two of the dairies (a chronic compliance issue not currently in dispatch).
