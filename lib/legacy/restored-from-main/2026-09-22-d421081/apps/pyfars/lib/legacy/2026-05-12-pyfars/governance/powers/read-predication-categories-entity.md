The theory-derivation-officer and the self-theory-author may both read any entity in `theories/.../predication-categories/` without incurring a duty and without the substrate having any claim against either officer for doing so.
This is a Hohfeldian privilege: the officer has a liberty; the substrate has a no-right.

The privilege exists because both offices' core functions depend on reading predication-categories material.
The theory-derivation-officer's Phases 1–5 all begin by consulting the math floor; the self-theory-author lifts upstream patterns from predication-categories into PyFARS-side concepts/objects/claims.
If reading the math floor required per-entity authorization, neither loop could function.

## What this privilege is not

It is not a write privilege.
The theory-derivation-officer may not write to predication-categories.
When a derivation reveals a gap or error in predication-categories, the officer must stop and file a triage note (`write-triage-note` power, `escalate-when-theory-gap-found` duty).
Writing into predication-categories is outside the theory-derivation-officer's scope — that is work for the `mathematical-researcher` agent or the `predication-category-theory-researcher` role.

## Correlation

The correlate is a `no-right` on the passive party (the substrate / the predication-categories maintainer).
No agent can claim the theory-derivation-officer is obligated to refrain from reading.
The reading is normatively unconstrained.
