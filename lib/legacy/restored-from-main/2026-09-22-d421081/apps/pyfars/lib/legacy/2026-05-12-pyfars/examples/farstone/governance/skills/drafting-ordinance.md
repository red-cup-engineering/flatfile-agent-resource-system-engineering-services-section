---
name: "Drafting ordinance"
description: "Council member action that drafts a proposed ordinance for council consideration, with text, findings, and effective date. Use when proposing new local law."
---


## Requirements

- A council member's authority
- A subject area (zoning, public-health, fiscal, etc.)
- Charter compatibility check (no clauses conflict with hard-core C1-C5)

## Steps

1. Draft the ordinance text with sections numbered.
2. Include findings (the problem the ordinance addresses) and an effective date.
3. File the draft at `governance/decisions/ordinances/draft-<slug>.md` with status: draft.
4. Schedule first reading on the next regular council agenda.

## Outcomes

The draft ordinance record exists; the council agenda includes its first reading; the city clerk's public-registry channel announces the upcoming reading.
