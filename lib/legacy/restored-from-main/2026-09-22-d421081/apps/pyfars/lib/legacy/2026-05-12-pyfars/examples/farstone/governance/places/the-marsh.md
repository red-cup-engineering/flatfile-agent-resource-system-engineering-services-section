The salt marsh occupying the island's northeast corner — ~3 km² of tidal grass, channels, and brackish flats.
Spartina-dominated; bird-rich; the soft-shell clam bed and the state-listed common-tern breeding colony are both here.

The shellfish bed is closed to harvest during summer warm spells (water temperature + recent rainfall trigger the closure); a closure rule under [`objects/`](../../objects/) (forthcoming) writes `(the-marsh, shellfish-closed, true|false) = T` from the relevant cells, and the records clerk's office publishes the closure to the public registry.

The Marsh boundary with [`marsh-end`](marsh-end.md) is contested in places — sea-level events have moved the high-water line by tens of meters in some sections.
A parcels-near-marsh map is updated after major coastal storms.

The Marsh is managed under a state shoreline-protection designation; the city council's authority over the Marsh is limited to public access and no-build zones inside its sliver of jurisdiction.
