The lighthouse at Farstone Island's southern tip, marking the approach to Farston Harbor and the Farr River bar.
Established 1872, automated since 1987; structure and lamp maintained by the mainland Coast Guard, surrounding parcel owned by the town.

South Point Light is referenced in Farstone's emergency dispatch — when an offshore-distress witness arrives at the harbormaster's office, the dispatcher's procedure includes consulting the light's visibility cell (currently a manual log; eventually a derived cell from weather + visibility).

The lighthouse's surrounding parcel is leased to the Farstone Historical Society for a small museum, open seasonally.
