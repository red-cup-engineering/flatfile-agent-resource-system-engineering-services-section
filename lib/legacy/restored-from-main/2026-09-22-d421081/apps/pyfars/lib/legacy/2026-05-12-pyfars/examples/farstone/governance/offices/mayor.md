The chief executive of Farston.
Holds appointment power over department directors (subject to council confirmation), veto power over council resolutions (overrideable by 4 of 5 council votes), and represents the city in dealings with the state, neighboring municipalities, and federal agencies.

## Powers

- **Sign ordinances into law.** Council-passed ordinances become effective when the mayor signs them or when the mayor's 10-day veto window expires unsigned.
- **Veto council resolutions.** A vetoed resolution returns to council; 4 of 5 council votes override the veto.
- **Appoint department directors.** Subject to council confirmation by majority vote.
- **Convene emergency session.** When public health, safety, or property is threatened, the mayor may convene the council outside its regular schedule.

## Duties

- **Uphold the charter.** Acts contrary to the charter (Article I, Article III) are voidable.
- **Publish annual state-of-city.** Filed to the records clerk by January 31 each year.

## Immunity

Official acts performed in good faith within the scope of office are immune from civil suit per state municipal-immunities statute.
