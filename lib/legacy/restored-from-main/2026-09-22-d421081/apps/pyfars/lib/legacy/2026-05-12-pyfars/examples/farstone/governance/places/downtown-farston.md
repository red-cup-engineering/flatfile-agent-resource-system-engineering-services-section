The central business district along Main Street — Farston city hall (the converted Victorian), the records clerk's office, the volunteer fire station, the post office, the public library, the town's general store and small businesses.
Most administrative dispatch is downtown-resident: the records clerk, finance director, mayor, and council all hold offices here.

Residential blocks back the commercial strip on three sides; population density is the island's highest.
The pothole at the intersection of Elm Street and 3rd Avenue (citizen complaint #2026-117) is in this neighborhood.

Hydrant-23, at the corner of 5th and Walnut, falls within downtown's water-distribution zone.
