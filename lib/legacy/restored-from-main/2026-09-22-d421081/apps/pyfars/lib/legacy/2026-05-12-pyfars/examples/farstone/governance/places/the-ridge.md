The town's neighborhood on Mt Farstone's southwest shoulder — residential lots stair-stepping up the slope from the upper end of downtown to about 200 m above sea level, beyond which the watershed-protection zone forbids new building.
About 800 residents in larger-than-downtown lots, partly wooded.

The Ridge is the source of the town's distance-water-pressure problem: the upper streets sit near the gravity-feed limit of the municipal system, and Public Works keeps a small pump station at Ridge-and-Mountainview that boosts pressure for the upper blocks.

The Mt Farstone trailhead is at the top of Ridge Road; the trail itself is informal beyond the trailhead.
