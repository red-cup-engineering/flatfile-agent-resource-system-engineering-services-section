---
name: "Preparing draft budget"
description: "Compiles departmental requests, prior-year actuals, and revenue forecasts into a draft FY budget for council adoption. Use during the annual budget cycle, with the draft due to council by April 15."
---


## Requirements

- Prior-year actuals and the current-year reconciliation (through Q3)
- Department-by-department budget requests
- Revenue forecast from the city's revenue model
- Authorization from the finance-director office

## Steps

1. Aggregate department requests; flag any request exceeding 110% of prior-year actual.
2. Apply revenue forecast as the binding constraint; balance to revenue.
3. Write the draft budget to `governance/decisions/draft-budget-fy<year>.md` with line items, revenue forecast, and contingency-line size.
4. File to the council for review at least 60 days before the June 15 adoption deadline.
5. Witness the filing.

## Outcomes

A draft budget record exists.
The council has 60 days for review and amendments.
On adoption, the draft moves to `governance/decisions/budget-fy<year>.md`.
