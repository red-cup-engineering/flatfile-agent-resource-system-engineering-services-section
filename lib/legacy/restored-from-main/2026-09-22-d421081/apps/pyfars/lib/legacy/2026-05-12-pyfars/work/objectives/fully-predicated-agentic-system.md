PyFARS satisfies all five commitments of a *predicated agentic computing system*:

1. Predication substrate (four-valued bilattice, closure-rule reasoning)
2. First-class provenance and witnessing
3. Agential self-generation (every operative element is a substrate entity)
4. Federation by value-domain join
5. Practical reasoning as substrate operator schedule

The campaign [`ship-pyfars-autonomous-loop`](../campaigns/ship-pyfars-autonomous-loop.md) is the active initiative toward this objective.
