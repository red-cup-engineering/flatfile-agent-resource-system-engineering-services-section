---
name: "Declaring fire watch"
description: "Fire-chief action that declares a fire-watch condition (heightened readiness, restricted activities) for the city or a specific area. Use during high-risk weather, parade days, or after fire-detection-system failures."
---


## Requirements

- Fire-chief authority
- A stated condition (red-flag warning, parade, system-down)
- Defined scope (city-wide or specific zones)

## Steps

1. Issue the fire-watch declaration with the trigger condition and the scope.
2. Notify the public through the records-clerk's public-registry channel.
3. Activate restrictions per the fire code (open-flame ban, additional patrols).
4. File the declaration at `governance/decisions/fire-watches/<watch-slug>.md`.

## Outcomes

The fire-watch is in force; the public registry has the notice; restrictions apply until rescinded.
