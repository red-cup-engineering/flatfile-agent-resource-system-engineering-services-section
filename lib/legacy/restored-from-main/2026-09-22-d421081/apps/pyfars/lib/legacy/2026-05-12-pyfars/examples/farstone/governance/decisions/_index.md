# Farston decisions

Council-adopted resolutions, mayoral signings, finance authorizations, and other settled committed decisions.

## Layout

- `permits/` — building-permit decisions (issued by building-safety-director)
- `minutes/` — published council meeting minutes (filed by records clerk)
- `budget-flags/` — budget overrun flags (filed by finance director)
- top-level: ordinances and council resolutions
