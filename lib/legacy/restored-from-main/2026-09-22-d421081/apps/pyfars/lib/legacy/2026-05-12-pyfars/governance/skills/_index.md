Skill records define executable procedures that power exercises enable.
Each skill has a gerunding verb phrase as its name and a two-sentence description: what changes when the skill runs, and when to use it.

Skills are the operational complement of powers: powers grant standing to act; skills define how to act.
An office's `provides-capability:` list names the skills its incumbent can execute.
