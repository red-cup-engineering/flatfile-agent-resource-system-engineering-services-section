Open questions for the captain or the theory stack.
Questions live here when:

- A governance record cannot be completed because a theoretical foundation is missing or unclear.
- An operational decision requires captain input before the governance can settle.
- A pattern in the governance records suggests a theory question upstream (in PredWork, predicated-governance, predicated-computing, or predication-categories) that the theory officer should route to the appropriate researcher.

A question that gets answered moves to a decision record (`../decisions/`) or is encoded in the relevant governance record.
A question that reveals an upstream theory gap gets filed in the appropriate theory's `questions/` directory by the theory officer.
