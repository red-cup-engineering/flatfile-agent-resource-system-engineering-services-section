The rural strip along the island's north coast — scattered homes on long lots running back from the shore, separated by stands of conifer-broadleaf forest and intermittent streams.
Population about 320.

North Shore is the part of the island farthest from the town's emergency response capacity; the volunteer fire department's mutual-aid agreement with the mainland county covers north-shore incidents when town units can't make response window.
The mainland fireboat is the primary reach for north-shore marine emergencies.

There are no public hydrants on the north shore; firefighting water is drafted from the streams or the sea.
This is a known infrastructure gap, periodically discussed at council and never funded.
