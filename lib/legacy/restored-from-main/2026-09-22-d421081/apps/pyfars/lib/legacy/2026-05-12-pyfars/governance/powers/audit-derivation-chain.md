The theory officer may run a governance-layer coverage audit of the theory-derivation loop at any time.
The community has a liability: audit findings are substrate-binding — a degeneration signal produced by this power cannot be selectively ignored; it must receive a steward response or a documented counter-reading.

This power enables the `auditing-derivation-chain-governance` skill.
It is distinct from the theory-derivation-officer's `auditing-theory-derivation-coverage` power (Phase 1 of the derivation loop): that power starts a derivation cycle; this power assesses the governance health of the entire loop from outside.

## Scope of the audit

The audit produced by exercising this power covers:

1. **Loop activity**: are Phase 1 triage notes appearing at the expected cadence?
2. **Aim-set dynamics**: is Γ refilling the aim-set after aims complete?
3. **Goal-cell alignment**: are active task goals aligned with C1–C5?
4. **Upstream gap backlog**: are unresolved theory-gap triage notes accumulating?

The audit is a *governance* product, not a *derivation* product.
It reads the derivation outputs and assesses their pattern; it does not produce new derivation content.

## Duty that attaches

- `maintain-derivation-chain-integrity` fires as a standing obligation requiring this power be exercised on each derivation cycle.
  The power is not merely permitted; its exercise is required by the standing duty.
