---
title: "Close an execution loop on a dispatched task"
type: jural-relation
kind: power
active-holder: pyfars-executive-officer
passive-party: pyfars-community
scope-action: mark-dispatched-task-as-closed
correlate-of: liability
theory-basis: [jural-relation, normative-operational-layering, task-cell-system]
---


The XO may classify the outcome of a dispatched task: completed, stalled, reassigned, or escalated.
This settles the task's closure cell and discharges the XO's `close-execution-loops` duty for that task.
The passive party has a liability: the closure classification takes normative effect and updates the task's status in the substrate.

A task dispatched by the XO that has no closure classification is an unfulfilled duty.
Escalation is a valid closure: it moves the task to a higher authority (steward or captain) with a witness recording why escalation was necessary.
