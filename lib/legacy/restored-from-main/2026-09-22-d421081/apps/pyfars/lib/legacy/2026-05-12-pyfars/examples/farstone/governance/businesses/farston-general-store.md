The longstanding general store on Main Street — groceries, hardware basics, post office substation, propane exchange, and the unofficial bulletin board for the town.
Open seven days a week with extended Friday and Saturday hours.
Stays open most holidays (the proprietor's choice; ferry days and bad-weather days both bring customers).

The store's `open-now` cell is read by the homepage at [`farston-web`](../assets/computing/web/farston-web/farston-web.md) to render the open/closed badge in the businesses panel.
