The XO may assign a ready task to an eligible office, settling the assignment cell on the task record.
The assigned office has a liability: an O→N rule fires, settling a duty cell on the office's incumbent — the office is now obligated to complete or formally return the task.

Distinct from `queue-intent`: dispatch is a definite assignment to a specific office; queuing is a request for the heartbeat to schedule the work.

Triggers the `close-execution-loops` duty: the XO must watch for a completion or stall event on every task it dispatches.
