The island's only health facility — two physicians, three nurses, on-call rotation.
After-hours and weekend emergencies route to the mainland county hospital; the standard transfer is the morning ferry, and air-evac for time-critical cases.
The volunteer fire department doubles as the EMS transport service.

The clinic is the canonical reporter for the (forthcoming) public-health contagion-thresholds rule; cases that exceed reporting thresholds produce witnesses that the records-clerk publishes to the public registry within the charter's transparency window.
