A standing duty incurred by holding the steward office.
The steward is ultimately accountable for preventing degeneration of PyFARS as an enterprise.
The theory officer detects degeneration signals and reports them; the steward must act on those reports.
The duty of detection is the theory officer's; the duty of response is the steward's.

## Why this is the steward's duty

Per `enterprise` and `military-command-chain-as-modal-composition`, the steward's accountability for C and Γ is an $H^0_t$-invariant — it does not redistribute downward through the command chain.
The theory officer can observe degeneration and report it; the XO can route the report; but only the steward can take the governance actions that address degeneration (amending C, reconstituting O, redirecting the theory-derivation loop).
The duty sits at the level where the authority to respond also sits.

## The three responses

The three degeneration modes from `enterprise` have corresponding steward responses:

1. **Exhaustion of positive heuristic**: if Γ has genuinely run through the current hard core's implications, the steward must assess whether to widen C (a novation, amending the hard-core commitments to open new research directions) or to acknowledge that PyFARS has reached a natural completion point.
  The latter is rare; the former is the expected response when the theory program is still active at a level the hard core was too narrow to exploit.

2. **Institutional corruption**: if the cell-pattern shows the operative structure dominating — work shifting from internal goods to external goods — the steward must reconstitute O: dissolving offices that have drifted from their governance purpose, installing new personnel accountable to C, or adjusting the XO's dispatch priorities to rebalance the aim-set toward charter-aligned work.

3. **Closure of domain**: if the predication-categories research program has genuinely closed, PyFARS as an aim-generative enterprise may be complete.
  The steward's response is to formally acknowledge completion, produce a novation record terminating the enterprise as currently constituted, and either dissolve the enterprise or reconstitute it with a new hard core appropriate to whatever work follows.

## Scope of this duty

The steward responds to degeneration signals; it does not produce them.
Proactively reading the theory officer's reports, the coverage audits, and the task goal-cell patterns is good stewardship but is not separately mandated by this duty — that ongoing monitoring belongs to the theory officer's `maintain-derivation-chain-integrity` duty.

## Enforcement

Pre-M5: manual.
The captain can audit whether degeneration signals filed by the theory officer have received steward responses within a reasonable time.
Unanswered degeneration-signal triage notes older than one derivation cycle are evidence of an unfulfilled duty.

Post-M5: the closure rule system checks that degeneration-signal triage notes with `requires-action: steward-review` have downstream steward-response records.
Missing response produces a governance-rule diagnostic attributing the gap to the steward office.
