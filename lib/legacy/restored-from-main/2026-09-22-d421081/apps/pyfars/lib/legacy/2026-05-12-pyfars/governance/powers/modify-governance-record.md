Admits the holder to edit the frontmatter or body of an existing file under `governance/`.
Covers decisions, conventions, charter, office records, power records, duty records, and skill records.

## Attached duties

`consult-predwork-before-encoding` fires before any use of this power.
For modifications that change the schema or scope of a record, the same theory-derivation review applies as for creation.

## Does not cover

- Creating new governance records (separate power: `create-governance-record`).
- Modifying work records (separate power: `modify-work-record`).
