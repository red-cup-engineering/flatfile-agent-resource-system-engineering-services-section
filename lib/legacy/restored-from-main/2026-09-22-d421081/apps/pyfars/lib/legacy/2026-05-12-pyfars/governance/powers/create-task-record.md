Admits the holder to create a new file under `work/tasks/<area>/`.
Tasks are concrete, single-sitting implementation units.
They inherit their theory grounding from the goal they serve — they do not need independent `theory-basis:` derivation, but must be linked to a goal that has one.

## Attached duties

- `consult-predwork-before-encoding` (verify task schema against conventions before writing).

## Does not require

- `derive-from-theory-before-planning` — tasks derive their grounding from their parent goal, not independently.

## Enables

- Skill: `writing-task-records`
