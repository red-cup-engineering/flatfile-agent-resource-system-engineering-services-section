The steward may add a new office to PyFARS's operative structure O.
The community has a liability: once created, the office's structural cells are substrate content — settling-monotonicity means those cells only accumulate.
A created office can be dissolved (a separate power), but its creation record persists.

Per `office-as-double-settlement`, a new office is a substrate entity $O$ with structural cells (normative-structure $P$, action-domain $A_O$, deontic-profile $D_O$).
The steward's exercise of this power produces these cells in the operations settling.

## Constraints on exercise

A new office must:

1. Have a defined `scope` — what layer of the enterprise it operates at.
2. Have a defined `action-domain` — no office can be created without articulating what actions it governs.
3. Have a theory-basis — the office's structure must be derivable from predicated-governance's `office-as-double-settlement` and the relevant PredWork theory objects.
4. Not overlap the `amend-hard-core`, `create-office`, `dissolve-office`, `install-personnel`, `revoke-installation`, or `ratify-governance-rule` powers unless the steward explicitly delegates those powers via `install-personnel` with bounded scope.
  The steward's constitutional powers are not inheritable by design.

## No duties attach at creation

Creating an office does not immediately generate duties — those attach when a power is exercised by an incumbent.
However, the created office's `deontic-profile` defines what duties WILL attach when its powers are exercised.
The steward should define the deontic profile carefully at creation time; post-creation changes require `modify-governance-record`.
