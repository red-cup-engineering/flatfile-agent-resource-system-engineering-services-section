Working-waterfront cafe at the foot of Harbor Way.
Open early for fishermen and ferry crew, dinner Friday and Saturday in season.
The owner's a former lobster captain; the menu is short and seafood-heavy.

When the harbormaster issues a storm-closure witness on the harbor, the cafe is the first place that fills with stranded ferry passengers; the cafe's `open-now` cell carries an extra `extended-for-storm: true` cell on those days, written by the cafe's licensee in response to the closure (a forthcoming runbook composes the steps).
