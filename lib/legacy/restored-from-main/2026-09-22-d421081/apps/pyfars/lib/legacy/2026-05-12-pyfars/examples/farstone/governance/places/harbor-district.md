The working waterfront on the south side of town — ferry terminal, fish-pier, harbormaster's office, recreational moorings, three boatyards, a chandlery, and the harbor-side cafés and outfitters that cater to ferry passengers and tourists in season.
The Farr River meets the sea at the harbor's western edge.

The harbormaster is a town-appointed office (forthcoming as a record); they coordinate with the mainland Coast Guard on matters above the high-tide line and with Public Works on the working harbor's infrastructure.

The ferry terminal sits at the eastern end of the harbor; the ferry runs to the mainland twice daily, weather-dependent.
Tide-state cells govern berth depth at low water; severe storm cells trigger a temporary closure witness via the harbormaster's office.
