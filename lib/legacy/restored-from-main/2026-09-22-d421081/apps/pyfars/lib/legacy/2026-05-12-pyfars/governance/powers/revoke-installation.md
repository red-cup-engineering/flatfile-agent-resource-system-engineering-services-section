The steward may revoke a personnel installation, ending an incumbent's dignitas state.
Revocation drives the incumbent's incumbency cells from $\mathbf{t}$ toward $\top$ and then to $\mathbf{f}$ via the substrate's repair machinery — per `delegation-as-authority-residuation`'s revocability invariant (row 4 of the residuation table: $\top \Rightarrow x$ does not produce $\mathbf{t}$, ensuring the revocation assertion, once made, cannot be transparently overridden by re-grant).

The capability-propagation N→O rule fires in reverse: when the incumbent's recognition and induction cells drop below $\mathbf{t}$, the capability section update is no longer triggered.
The office's capabilities are no longer attributed to that incumbent.

## Revocation does not erase the record

Per settling-monotonicity, the historical record of the incumbent's tenure — task completion records, classification witnesses, any theory content produced — is not retracted.
Revocation asserts a new cell; it does not modify prior cells.
The substrate preserves the full tenure record alongside the revocation increment.

## Grounds for revocation

The steward exercises this power when:

- An incumbent has acted outside their office's `action-domain` in a way that cannot be corrected.
- An incumbent's capacity for incumbency has changed (e.g., an LLM agent whose endpoint is deprecated, a human who is no longer able to serve).
- Restructuring of O requires re-staffing offices with different personnel.

Revocation for cause (a charter violation by the incumbent) should be accompanied by a `witness-hard-core-amendments` record if the violation touched C, or a governance-decision record in `governance/decisions/` if it involves a guideline-tier matter.

## Revocation without immediate succession

An office may be left vacant after revocation — both required predicates at $\mathbf{f}$ for all candidates.
Vacancy means the office's powers are not exercisable until a new installation.
Extended vacancy of a governing office (steward, XO, theory-officer) is an operational risk the steward should address promptly via `install-personnel`.
