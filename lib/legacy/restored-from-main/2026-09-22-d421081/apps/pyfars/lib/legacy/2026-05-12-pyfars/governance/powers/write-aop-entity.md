The theory-derivation-officer may create or modify an entity in the `theories/.../agential-orchestration-platform/` theory layer.
This is Phase 3 (Lift) of the theory-derivation loop: lifting predicated-computing entities to the AOP surface, making them accessible to the programmer working at the FARS spec level.

Scoped to Phase 3.
Exercising this power in Phase 2 or Phase 4 is a `write-in-correct-derivation-layer` duty violation.

## Relationship to write-predicated-computing-entity

AOP entities derive from predicated-computing entities.
The theory-derivation-officer writes a predicated-computing entity (Phase 2) before lifting it to AOP (Phase 3).
Writing an AOP entity that lacks a corresponding predicated-computing basis is a layer violation — the lift has no substrate to lift from.

## Duty that attaches

- `write-in-correct-derivation-layer` fires: the write must land in `theories/.../agential-orchestration-platform/` and the active phase must be Phase 3.
- `escalate-when-theory-gap-found` fires if lifting reveals a gap in the predicated-computing layer.
  Stop; file the gap upstream; do not synthesize predicated-computing content from within the AOP layer.
