---
name: "Convening emergency session"
description: "Mayoral action that calls the city council into emergency session outside the regular meeting calendar. Use during declared emergencies or when matters cannot wait for the next regular meeting."
---


## Requirements

- Mayoral authority
- A stated emergency (charter Article II §3 grounds: public-health, fiscal-responsibility, public-safety)
- At least 24-hour notice to council members (or shorter if all 5 consent)

## Steps

1. Issue a written call specifying the emergency and the agenda restricted to it.
2. Notify each council member through their preferred channel.
3. File the call notice in `operations/emergency-sessions/<session-slug>.md`.
4. Convene at the noticed time; sessions outside the regular calendar are limited to the noticed agenda.

## Outcomes

The emergency-session record exists; council members are notified; the meeting proceeds with restricted agenda.
