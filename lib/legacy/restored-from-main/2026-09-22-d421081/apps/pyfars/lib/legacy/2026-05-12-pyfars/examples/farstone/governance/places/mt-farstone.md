The central peak of the island — 620 m at the summit, granite outcrops above the treeline, mixed conifer-broadleaf forest below.
The mountain's catchment is the source of the Farr River and of every well-fed freshwater source on the island; logging on its flanks is restricted by ordinance.

The municipal water-supply ordinance designates the upper-flanks forest a watershed-protection zone; building permits within the zone are routed through Public Works for hydrologic review in addition to the standard Building & Safety review.

The summit and ridgeline are reached by a marked trail leaving from the upper end of [`the-ridge`](the-ridge.md); the trailhead is town-maintained, the trail itself is informal.
