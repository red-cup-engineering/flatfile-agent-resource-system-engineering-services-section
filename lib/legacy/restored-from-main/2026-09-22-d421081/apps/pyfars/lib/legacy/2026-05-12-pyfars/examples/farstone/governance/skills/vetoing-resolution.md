---
name: "Vetoing resolution"
description: "Mayoral action that vetoes a passed council resolution, returning it to the council with the mayor's stated objections. Use when the mayor disagrees with a council resolution."
---


## Requirements

- A council-passed resolution within the 10-day window
- Mayoral authority
- Stated grounds for the veto

## Steps

1. Document the grounds for veto with reference to the resolution.
2. Write the veto record to `governance/decisions/vetoes/<resolution-slug>.md`.
3. Return the resolution to council with the veto attached.
4. Council may override with a 4-of-5 supermajority within 30 days.

## Outcomes

The veto record exists; the resolution returns to council with the mayor's objections; the override clock starts.
