The five-member legislative body.
Adopts ordinances, approves the annual budget, confirms mayoral appointments, and hears citizen petitions.
Members are elected at-large to staggered four-year terms.

## Powers (collective)

- **Pass ordinance.** Adopted by majority vote at a regular meeting; effective when signed by the mayor or when veto window expires.
- **Approve budget.** Adopted by June 15 each year per Charter Article III §5.
- **Confirm appointment.** Mayoral appointments take effect when confirmed by majority council vote.
- **Override veto.** Requires 4 of 5 council votes within 30 days of the veto.

## Duties

- **Hear petitions** with 50+ signatures at the next regular meeting per Charter Article III §2.
- **Meet publicly** per Charter Article I C3.
- **Publish minutes within 5 days** of each meeting; filed to the records clerk.

## Quorum

Three members.
No motion passes without quorum (Charter Article III §1).
