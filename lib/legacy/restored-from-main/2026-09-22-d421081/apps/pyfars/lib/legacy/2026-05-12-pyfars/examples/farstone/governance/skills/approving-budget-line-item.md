---
name: "Approving budget line-item"
description: "Council action that approves a single line-item of a draft or amended budget, recorded as a vote with affirming, dissenting, and abstaining tallies. Use during budget hearings or amendments."
---


## Requirements

- A budget line-item in a draft or amended budget record
- A council member's voting authority (city-council office)
- Quorum (per Charter Article II §2: at least 3 of 5 members present)

## Steps

1. Read the draft budget record and locate the line-item.
2. Cast a vote (affirming/dissenting/abstaining) on the line-item.
3. Record the vote in `governance/decisions/budget-line-items/`.
4. When the line-item reaches majority affirming, the line is "approved" and locks for the budget cycle.

## Outcomes

The line-item carries its tally cell; an approval witness fires when the threshold is met; downstream finance-director may authorize spend against the line.
