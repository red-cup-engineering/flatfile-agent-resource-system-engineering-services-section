The municipal town that occupies the harbor side of Farstone Island — ~10,400 of the island's ~12,000 residents, the seat of the city council and dept directors, the converted-Victorian city hall on Main Street, the volunteer fire department, the Farston water treatment plant.
The remaining ~1,600 islanders live in [`farr-valley`](farr-valley.md), [`north-shore`](north-shore.md), [`the-ridge`](the-ridge.md), and [`marsh-end`](marsh-end.md).

The town's chartered jurisdiction extends to the high-tide line of [`farston-harbor`](farston-harbor.md).
Land-use authority over the rest of the island is incorporated through county-style records (forthcoming).

Neighborhoods within the town:

- [`downtown-farston`](downtown-farston.md) — Main Street, city hall, central business district.
- [`harbor-district`](harbor-district.md) — working waterfront, ferry terminal, recreational moorings.
- (The other named neighborhoods — [`marsh-end`](marsh-end.md), [`the-ridge`](the-ridge.md) — are within the town but contiguous with the rural parts of the island.)
