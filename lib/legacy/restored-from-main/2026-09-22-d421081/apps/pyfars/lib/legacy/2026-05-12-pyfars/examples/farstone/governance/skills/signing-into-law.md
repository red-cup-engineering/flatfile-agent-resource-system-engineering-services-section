---
name: "Signing into law"
description: "Mayoral action that signs a council-passed ordinance into law, settling its enforceability. Use after a passed ordinance reaches the mayor's desk."
---


## Requirements

- An ordinance passed by majority of the city council
- Mayoral authority
- Within the 10-day signing window (per Charter Article II §4); after 10 days an unsigned-and-unvetoed ordinance becomes law automatically

## Steps

1. Review the passed ordinance.
2. Sign and date the ordinance.
3. Update the ordinance record's `signed-at` cell.
4. The ordinance's `effective-date` cell becomes T at the date specified in the ordinance.

## Outcomes

The ordinance is law; the public registry shows the signed status; enforcement may proceed per the ordinance's effective date.
